01 // THE CREATOR

HUMAN, BEHIND THE MACHINE

Long before writing code, my life was measured in hundredths of a second. Spending ten years on starting blocks as a National Swimmer and Gold Medalist taught me that discipline is a daily standard, not a switch you flip. Today, as an AI Engineer with a Computer Science background (4.0 CGPA), I bring that same obsession with precision to software. I build the systems that sit beneath the AI APIs, from high-performance RAG pipelines on pgvector to async ingestion in production.

Beyond the code, the builder instinct came before the job title. While competing as a swimmer, I noticed our federation was running entirely on paper, no databases, no tracking, no system. So I built one. That led to serving as Data and Automation Engineer at the Pakistan Swimming Federation, digitizing records for 200+ athletes and running the technical side of national championships. When I step away from the terminal, you will find me at the chessboard training the same tactical foresight and pattern recognition I rely on to architect software.
